Hey there! This file is so you know what you expect when you contribute towards this project and what you should and shouldn't do

## DCO Sign off

When making a commit, they must be signed off and agree to our DCO (you can find it in the `DCO.md` file) otherwise your PR will be rejected. You can do this my appending `-s` when making commits. If you forget to sign off your commits, you can sign off them by rebasing your commits with `--signoff`

## Licence agreement

All contributions made towards this project will be licences under the Apache License 2.0. You agree that you have the rights to licence your code under this licence.

## Code of Conduct

When contributing, whether that be issues, PRs or other methods, you agree to our Code of Conduct which can be read in the `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` file

## Trademark and copyright notice

Remember that contributing to this project does **not grant you trademark rights or copyright** for use of any kind such as logo or name for use in your own products. More information can be found in the ending of the `README.md` file with the heading 'Branding Rights'

## AI Usage

You may use AI within the development of contributions however, you must disclose that AI was used and which specific AI model (via the Assisted-by Git tag and PR descriptions). You agree that you take full responsibility and accountability towards the resulting code and assets. AI agents (AI controlling it's own seperate account) are prohibited unless another human openly and knowingly takes accountability and responsibility for it's actions.

## PR Reviews

When making a PR, you'll need to get at least one other person with write permissions to `main` branch to approve your PR for it to merge. If your PR is rejected, we may give you the ability to get it re-reviewed provided you make the changes requested by the reviewer.

## Protected branches

Please don't push to main (only Splamei can do that)!! Instead please fork the repo and make a PR to the correct branch that fits your and the project's goals.
